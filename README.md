Problem Statement:

Retail fashion businesses generate large amounts of transaction data, but extracting actionable insights from this data can be challenging.

The key business questions addressed in this project are:

Which product categories generate the highest revenue? How do customer demographics influence sales? Are discounts and promo codes increasing revenue? What seasonal trends affect purchasing behavior?

WorkFlow:

Data Collection Retail fashion sales data collected from customer transactions.
Data Cleaning Removed duplicates. Checked for missing values. Standardized categorical values.
Data Transformation Created calculated measures for revenue analysis. Grouped customers into age categories. Aggregated data for seasonal and category-level analysis.
Dashboard Development
Developed an interactive dashboard containing:

KPIs Bar Charts Trend Analysis Slicers and Filters Customer Behavior Metrics

Insights:

Clothing contributes the highest revenue. Outerwear contributes the lowest revenue. Clothing accounts for nearly three times the revenue of Footwear. Adult customers generate the highest revenue. Male customers contribute significantly more revenue than female customers across all age groups. Young customers represent the smallest revenue segment. Revenue generated without discounts exceeds revenue generated with discounts. Customers appear willing to purchase even without promotional incentives.

Recommendations:

Revenue Growth Prioritize Clothing inventory and marketing campaigns. Expand successful Clothing product lines. Customer Segmentation Target Adult customers with personalized recommendations. Design campaigns to attract Young customers and increase engagement. Promotion Strategy Reduce unnecessary discounting. Use targeted promotions for specific customer segments rather than broad campaigns. Seasonal Planning Increase inventory before Fall season. Launch Summer promotional campaigns to boost low-season performance. Shipping Optimization Continue offering Free Shipping incentives. Promote Express Shipping upgrades. Customer Retention Implement loyalty rewards for repeat customers. Develop personalized offers based on purchase frequency. Payment Experience Optimize checkout experience for Credit Card, Venmo, and Cash customers. Expand support for popular digital payment options. Customer Satisfaction Monitor ratings below 3.5 for immediate corrective actions. Collect customer feedback to improve product quality and service.
